IMPORTANT BEFORE/AFTER DEPLOYMENT:
1. Deploy this folder as a Render Static Site. Publish directory: . (the folder containing index.html).
2. Once Render gives you the final public URL (or you connect a custom domain), create sitemap.xml containing the final absolute URLs for:
   /, /about.html, /services.html, /fares.html, /routes.html, /contact.html, /booking.html
3. Add this line to robots.txt after the final URL is known:
   Sitemap: https://YOUR-ACTUAL-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml
   Replace the example with your real HTTPS domain. Do not leave the placeholder.
4. Update the canonical URL and og:url tags on each page only after the final domain is known.
5. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console after the site is live.

BUSINESS DETAIL CHECK:
The schema uses Kolhapur, Maharashtra as the service area and does not invent a street address. Confirm the business name, phone, email and service area with the customer before publishing.
